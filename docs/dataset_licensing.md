# Dataset Licensing & Ethical Data Provenance

This document outlines the sources, copyrights, licenses, and privacy/ethical safeguards for all data assets utilized in this project.

---

## 1. Credit Card Fraud Detection Dataset (Task A)

- **Repository:** [Kaggle: `kartik2112/fraud-detection`](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kartik2112/fraud-detection)
- **Author:** Kartik Shenoy
- **Underlying Generator:** Sparkov Data Generation Tool by Brandon Harris
- **License:** **CC0: Public Domain Dedication**
  - *"To the extent possible under law, the creator has waived all copyright and related or neighboring rights to this work."*
- **Description:** A simulated transaction stream modeling real-world credit card purchasing behaviors across legitimate merchants and fraudulent attack vectors between January 1, 2019 and December 31, 2020.
- **Privacy & Ethical Safeguards:**
  - Zero Actual Consumer PII: Cardholder names, credit card numbers, addresses, and transaction identifiers are entirely synthetic.
  - No real financial institutions or individuals were compromised or exposed.

---

## 2. Loan Default Prediction Dataset (Task B)

- **Repository:** [Kaggle: `subhamjain/loan-prediction-based-on-customer-behavior`](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/subhamjain/loan-prediction-based-on-customer-behavior)
- **Author:** Subham Jain
- **License:** **CC0: Public Domain Dedication**
  - Free for academic, personal, and commercial research with no restrictions.
- **Description:** An empirical dataset capturing demographic, residential, and employment indicators for 252,000 credit applicants with labeled default histories.
- **Privacy & Ethical Safeguards:**
  - De-identified records: No Social Security Numbers (SSN), taxpayer identification numbers, bank account numbers, or direct personal addresses are present.
  - Fair Lending Compliance: Analysis in this project specifically audits occupational and residential features to detect proxy discrimination and bias under Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) standards.

## 3. Data Governance Statement

Both datasets are freely accessible under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication. No proprietary financial secrets, NDA-restricted datasets, or copyrighted materials are hosted in this repository.
