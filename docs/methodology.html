📐 Scientific Methodology & Cost Modeling

A formal mathematical derivation of expected loss minimization under asymmetric misclassification costs, leakage-free chronological partitioning protocols, and vectorized spatial-temporal feature engineering.

1. Derivation of Bayesian Minimum Expected Loss

Standard empirical risk minimization assumes symmetric binary loss (0-1 loss). In banking, we formulate decision theory over explicit cost matrices.

Let \( x \in \mathcal{X} \) denote an instance vector and \( Y \in \{0, 1\} \) the true ground-truth risk state. A decision rule \( d(x) \in \{0, 1\} \) incurs financial loss specified by the cost tensor \( C(d, Y) \):

  • \( C_{11} = C(TP) \): Operational cost of investigating a correctly caught risk.
  • \( C_{10} = C(FP) \): Friction cost of querying a legitimate transaction or applicant.
  • \( C_{01} = C(FN) \): Direct unrecovered financial charge-off or default write-down.
  • \( C_{00} = C(TN) \): Frictionless approval of legitimate transaction.

The conditional expected financial loss of action \( d=1 \) (predict risk / flag) versus \( d=0 \) (predict clean / approve) given posterior risk \( P(Y=1 \mid x) \) is:

\[ \mathcal{L}(d=1 \mid x) = C(TP) \cdot P(Y=1 \mid x) + C(FP) \cdot [1 - P(Y=1 \mid x)] \] \[ \mathcal{L}(d=0 \mid x) = C(FN) \cdot P(Y=1 \mid x) + C(TN) \cdot [1 - P(Y=1 \mid x)] \]

By the Bayes decision rule, we flag the transaction as positive whenever \( \mathcal{L}(d=1 \mid x) \le \mathcal{L}(d=0 \mid x) \). Solving for \( P(Y=1 \mid x) \) yields the exact analytical optimal threshold \( p^* \):

\[ p^* = \frac{C(FP) - C(TN)}{[C(FP) - C(TN)] + [C(FN) - C(TP)]} \]

Under normalized frictionless baselines (\( C(TN) = 0 \), \( C(TP) = 0 \)), this simplifies directly to:

\[ p^* = \frac{C(FP)}{C(FP) + C(FN)} \]

Because \( C(FN) \gg C(FP) \) in credit risk, the denominator dominates, mathematically guaranteeing that the loss-minimizing decision cutoff \( p^* \) lies substantially below 0.50.

3. Leakage Prevention: Strict Chronological Partitioning

Why standard K-Fold cross-validation invalidates financial risk evaluations through lookahead leakage.

In transaction streaming, random train/test splits violate causality: models train on future fraud patterns (e.g. December 2020) and evaluate on past transactions (e.g. March 2019). This creates artificial feature correlations and drastically inflates reported accuracy.

Training Partition: 2019 (100,000 Txns) → Chronological Boundary → Holdout Test Partition: 2020 (40,000 Txns)

To prevent statistical leakage:

  • All target encoders, standard scalers, and frequency mappers are encapsulated strictly inside scikit-learn Pipeline objects fit exclusively on training data.
  • No test sample statistics are ever seen during preprocessor fitting.
  • Threshold optimizer search is strictly fit on training/validation out-of-fold predictions.

4. Domain Feature Engineering Specifications

Extracting high-signal spatial, temporal, and credit solvency indicators.

Spatial: Great-Circle Haversine

Vectorized distance computation between cardholder residence coordinates \( (\text{lat}_1, \text{long}_1) \) and merchant POS terminal \( (\text{lat}_2, \text{long}_2) \):

\[ d = 2R \arcsin \left( \sqrt{\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) + \cos(\phi_1)\cos(\phi_2)\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \lambda}{2}\right)} \right) \]

Temporal: Cyclical Trigonometric

Mapping hour-of-day \( t \in [0, 23] \) to continuous circular coordinates preserving continuity between 23:59 and 00:00:

\[ \text{hour}_{\sin} = \sin\left(\frac{2\pi t}{24}\right), \quad \text{hour}_{\cos} = \cos\left(\frac{2\pi t}{24}\right) \]

Solvency: Experience & Stability

Deriving applicant employment stability ratio and continuous home equity interaction to quantify borrower vulnerability:

\[ \text{stability ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Job Years}}{\max(1, \text{Experience})}, \quad \text{income bracket} = \ln(1 + \text{Income}) \]
